# Humanoids
Humanoid creatures can be either people or monsters. Some monsters have a person form, while some are just monsters.

Creatures that only have a person form:
* Human
    * Humans like to enslave and exploit the other races so they are genuinely not trusted.
* Beast person
    * Half human, half animal; the animal is always a non-monster, which is why there is no monster form of a beast person.
    * There is a theory that all of the dragon beast people were killed by the dragons and dramore.
    * Dragon beast people do not have the ability to transform into dragons.
    * Some beast people are more human like, while others are more animal like. For example, turtle people and lion are animal like, while cat, wolf, and fox people are human like.
* Elf
    * Variants: high elf, snow elf, sea elf, dark elf, mud elf.
* Vampire
    * The vampire race is different from people who become vampires due to conversion (a.k.a. infection). If two converted vampires breed, their children are more vampiric. Thus, the vampire race is a unique race that can replace other races in a population if most of the population is already converted.
    * Holy, light, and water magic do the opposite and convert vampires into beast people if exposed constantly. However, ancient people believe beast people didn't generally originate from vampires, but very little is known about where the races originated from. The variant of beast person is based on both the current environment and the original races of the converted population.
* Dwarf
    * One foot shorter than a human.
    * The dwarves were created by the god of metal. Dwarf bodies slowly reinforce themselves with metal. This process takes decades, but the metal is fused into proteins using a combination of chemistry and natural magic. Similar methods are used to create flexible golems.
* Golem
    * A fully artificial being created by the ancient people.
    * They can only be found underground because an unknown organization has systematically destroyed all of them, likely out of fear of the powerful magic used to create them. The church says this is a good thing, but they have know nothing about the unknown organization.
* Lamard
    * Often described as a sea person, though that doesn't work as an unambiguous label.
    * Lamards are sometimes called larnasas as a derogatory term, but larnasas are an unrelated species of fish monster.
    * The lamard has oily, tough reptilian skin, and fins on its limbs for swimming. It's skin is very good at retaining water, making it easier for it to survive in deserts. Lamards can survive in a wide range of environments but can't breathe underwater.
* Mermaid
    * Often described as a fish person, though that doesn't work as an unambiguous label.
    * It is very differeent from a lamard. A mermaid has a fish half and a human half. The human half as slick oily skin. Mermaids dry out if outside the water for too long, and they can't move easily on land. Mermaids can breathe both underwater and in water due to their special gill-lung hybrid organs.
    * The mermaids hate all other races and are very territorial of their waters. It is said that they even control powerful sea mosnters and use them to keep boats and fishers out of their territory.

Creatures that have both a person and monster form:
* Goblin
    * It is believed that the person form has gone extinct.
    * The hobgoblin is actually a variant that is larger and less resilient to magic.
    * A goblin can grow into a general, lord, or king eventually. 
* Troll
    * The person form is often mistaken for beast people
    * They have small and large variants. The small variant is the size of a dwarf, and the large variant is 7 to 9 feet tall.
* Giant
    * The people form was driven extinct by the orcs.
* Orc
    * The monster form is only found deep in dungeons.
* Demon
    * The person form is also called dramore.
    * Demons often have the ability to control other monsters.
* Fairy
    * The person and monster version were driven extinct in ancient times, and no signs of either of them have been observed for millenia.
    * Fairies were said to be able to boost the magic power of others and were directly connected to the spirit realm, though they were not able to cast powerful spells themselves.
    * Spirits can sometimes be used in a similar way, but this is rare, and witches and certain objects are able to scare off these spirits. Black cats can scare off these spirits within around about a mile radius.

Creatures that only have a monster form:
* I should note that these creatures can still be intelligent.
* Cyclops
    * A cyclops can actually use its eye to cast spells. A soul node is instantly places at the point in space it is looking at and the spell is cast from the node, so mana does not need to move through the air from the eye to the casting location.
* Triclops
    * This creature is often seen in demon settlements and works together with them. The left eye (red) is for night vision, the right eye (blue) can see the chemical makeup of whatever it looks at, and the top eye can only see magical energy (it is almost pure white).
* Zorg
    * This monster is like a deformed zombie.
    * It is undead and has no living form, since it is born as an undead creature. It can infect people and creatures, turning them into zombies.
    * High level zorgs are very resistant to curses, vexes, and dark magic. Unlike other undead, which can often be easily handled with dark magic.
* Skelly
    * A skeleton with a random humanoid body. Often the bones of dead monsters, especially ones with powerful magic, assemble into skellies.
    * Most skellies have a humanoid shape, regardless of what kinds of bones they are assembled from. A skelly can also be assembled from the bones of many different animals, or from the broken pieces of bones. A skelly can also heal or grow stronger by combining new bones into its body or exchanging bones.
    * Skelly bones absorb the magic in their environment and become naturally enchanted with matching spells.
* Centaur
    * Centaurs have 4 legs, and thus 6 limbs in total.
    * Someon near the city of Wawalanike managed to actually tame a few centaurs, but centaurs generally hate people just like other monsters do.
    * There is a 4 armed variant of the centaur, which has 8 limbs in total.
    * Centaurs are omnivores but they mostly eat plants, since they have three stomachs.
* Humipede
    * Also known as the huamnoid centipede. This monster is made of 3 to 10 segments that are actually separate creatures.
    * The segments eat and digest food independently, and have to separate in order to urinate or excrete.
    * Their bodies are very different than humans, with a special structure on the back they use to link together. Which is kind of gross, but not as gross as you might think. When linked together, they use magic to accelerate their linked body. The linked body is primarily for combat and locomotion.
* Gorgon
    * Medusa was a monster with a spell cast from its eyes, but other gorgons are very different.
    * Each gorgon has one natural spell it can cast, and which body part the spell is cast from varies from by the gorgon.
    * The snakes on the heads of most gorgons have poisonous venom, but the type of snake varies. If any snakes are cut off, they grow back.
    * Gorgons have repitilian bodies, coming in three variants. The humanoid variant is a lot like a lamard (a fish person). The mermaid variant has a big long tail and doesn't actually look like a mermaid. It can use its tail to swim and also slither on it on the ground.

# Miscellaneous
Slime
* These monsters are made entirely of natural magic in the environment. Their element depends solely on the local mana.
* It is actually said that dragons originally started as slimes, since dragons are mostly made of magic.

Dragon
* Dragons are canivores and need to absorb quite a bit of magic to stay alive and active. However, they also have the ability to hiberneate, reducing energy usage to nearly zero. Some dragons are very old, which generally makes them very powerful and very scary. Killing these dragons is a top priority of the mage guild.
* Dragons do not have the ability to transform into dragon beast people. And not all dragons are intelligent. But the dragons that are intelligent often learn how to speak eventually, and can chant spells related to their natural spells and element.

Jumping spider
* A common variant of the spider that is huge, ranging from the size of a rabbit to the size of 2 or 3 people put together. The are usually able to jump of move with great force.

Flying squid
* A giant squid about the size of a teenager. It can fly using magic, and it uses its tentacles for combat. It has many variations, but it is usually incredibly flexible, and not something you want to fight from up close. It uses magic to hold the water in its body, so it is possible to kill it by drying it out, if you can overcome its natural magic resistance.
* There is a variant that shoots ball of water, ice, dirt, or rocks as an attack.
* When a flying squid sees a weak target, or weakens its prey, it will try to wrap its mouth around the targets head and use a spell to bite the head off and absorb the psychic and dark mana in head. Most creatures naturally have psychic mana in their brain, even if they don't have psychic abilities.
* There are no known cases of flying squid having psychic powers, even though some people are afraid that they do.
