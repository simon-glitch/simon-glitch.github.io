The game is set in 3D, and has a more traditional 3D combat system that mixes platforming, turn based attacks, real time combat, and bullet hell. Because why not honestly?

Also, the game doesn't require the player to know anything about Undertale going in. Instead it simply lays out its own world building and story as if it was its own game (and not derivative). However, in the opening room, I will add a sign directly crediting Toby Fox for his game and stating what I just said (that the player does not need prior knowledge).

Also, my game plays a lot more like an RPG. With quests and more dialogue options, and it's an open world 3D environment. But it will feel like an indie game since indie RPGs are just more fun.

People love relatable dialogue, so all the characters will say various helpful or relevant things. Like they might comment about how some indie games are designed like AAA games so there isn't much of a difference. And how no one knows what AAA means because no one can go to the human world. So how do they know so much about the above world? Well Sans can teleport up there XD. But also radio signals reach to the Underground, and random items noclip through the world and magically appear in the Underground. Oh and sometimes when a website or fandom dies, its soul becomes a monster in the Underground. Hehehe. All kinds of fun worldbuilding like that.

# Intro
In the intro you meet Flowey, but if you already have a run on your save, you can dodge his second attack. In any case, a slime will come and kill him. And heal you if needed. If you have met Simon in a past run, your character will realize the slime is Simon.

In general, you can meet Simon and ask him to accompany you on your journey. This can be used as a form of handicap mode. But Simon can also tell you how game mechanics work in more detail. If you want the info but not the handicap mode, you can get Simon's number and just call him. No matter which way you use Simon, you always get an encyclopedia GUI, rather than inefficient dialogue.

# Genocide
As I said, in the genocide route, a slime is the final boss. Simon has the attacks of all other enemies combined and is impossible to beat. Yes, impossible, literally. He will eventually destroy your determination, the game will say "Simon was stronger than your determination.", making you reset. In the second run, you have karma, and your determination has now been changed to have the tips be cyan, since Simon forcefully combined some of his determination into yours.

In the second run, you can kill Flowey in the intro. Also, the world has been poisoned by a calamity, that technically existed before you came here. Simon explains that you destroying the world in another timeline is what caused the calamity and that you will only make the next timeline worse. But he must stop you in this timeline, or you might destroy other worlds. This leaves your soul being even more cyan.

In the third run, you're not greeted by Flowey. Instead you are green by a dragon and forced to fight it with no gear. You have to use the environment of the room and any items you can find, so it's quite the tactcal rush. Then some rocks are destroyed, revealing the Hero's Sword. Once you pull it from the stone, you are given the option of the path or light or darkness.

If you choose the path of light, you will save the world from the calamity and do your best to atone for the sins of your past genocides. At the end of this path, you are asked if you will give in to the darkness. Either way, you have to fight the fusion of Sans and Asriel (Sansriel). This boss fight is custom designed to play like a 3D sonic game. If you gave into the darkness, Sansriel cannot be defeated, and he will banish you down into hell.

If you choose the path of darkness, you will do you best to erase all light and fill the world with darkness. At the end of this path, you are asked if you will give in to the darkness. If you do, you will have to fight Simon again. If you don't, Asriel will appear and absorb the darkness, and become Evil Asriel. Evil Asriel is even more powerful than God Asriel, since he has absorbed all of the LOVE from your two genocides. Simon will fuse with you though, to fight Evil Asriel, and together you will vanquish the darkness. In the post game, you can then vanquish the darkenss from within yourself. If you don't, you will still be in "third genoce mode" next run, which is only useful is you want to try the path of light immediately after the path of darkness. Otherwise, it's faster to vanquish the darkness within yourself before resetting.

Hell is actually an entirely separate world that then appears as a separate mod in the menu. It has its own save file, but you can link it back to the main save for the purpose of achievements. Hell has its own completely new world and story to it where you have to redeem yourself by making hell a better place, because as Mr. Sunglasses explains that Hell isn't what people think it is and people shouldn't have to suffer. Mr. Sunglasses is the king of Hell and he is there because he is just too cool for Nirvanna or Lalaland. Yes, those are the three places you can go to in the afterlife, but I'm not including the other two in-game.

# Failed genocide
If you fail to kill 100% of all life, but you kill most of it, a different genocide route happens, where you don't fight Simon. Instead, you go to the end of the world, and find a little girl, which dialogue from a voice in your head tells you is what's left of the world after you destroyed it all. She will be on a cliff. And dialogue will dare you to push her off. But then it will say monsters like you should be pushed off. Then the girl pushes you off the cliff, into the literally bottomless void, forcing you to reset. In this reset, the world will actively make it much more difficult for you to do a genocide route again. But if you do your best to kill as much as possible, you will find the girl at the end of the world again. This time she is a preteen. She will call you a horrible monster, and then the weight of your own sin and guilt will crush your soul and make you reset. You can repeat the process and she will be a teenager. This time she will fight you and say "If the pain doesn't kill me, it makes me stronger. You've made me stronger by trying to kill me.". In the next run, you won't be able to kill anything and will be directly forced to do the pacifist route.

# Pacifist
There is a Flowey fight at the end of the game on your first run, if you don't kill any major bosses, regardless of anything else you kill. My game boots you to the menu, rather than the desktop. It also says "Time to fight Flower" in the save menu, to minimize confusion. The Flowey fight focuses more on bullet hell / dodging than the rest of the game, but every time you die, the game makes it easier.

Beating Flowey unlocks the ability to start any new run with him dead or alive, and automatically leaves him dead. It also adds the option to start a fresh save with Flowey dead, for speedrunning / practice purposes. If you do a genocide or neutral run with Flowey dead, nothing is changed. If you do a pacifist run though, you get to fight Asriel, which gives you the True Ending. The True Ending unlocks the ability for you to visit the hall of memories (which I think is me copying a name from Stanlay Parable Deluxe Edition).

# Sunshine and rainbows
This is a little different than pacifist. If you complete all possible quests, and avoid killing quest related NPCs, you are given the option to fight Asriel in the arena and become king. Then if you completed all the major quests, the world is covered with magical sunshine and rainbows.

If you kill too many monsters though, or do too many bad deeds, or don't establish peace between the different areas, there will instead be a civil war, and you will be burned on a stake. After this, you're forced to reset. And Flower will greet you angrily complaining about how this isn't just a game for him and he resets with each reset too. Then he will eat you alive. Then you'll respawn and be forced to fight him, but this is a short fight against the weak plant version of Flowey. Flower disappears into the ground when you beat him and then Simon heals you and comments that he was really busy so he didn't have time to save you, regardless of whether you know the slime or not.
