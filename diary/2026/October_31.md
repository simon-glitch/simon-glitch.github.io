It's Halloween!



Minecraft Impossible Mode mod ideas:

* Hardcore mode
* Hostile mobs spawn with random buffs of infinite duration (each of these random chances is separate):

  * 50% chance for Invisibility
  * 45% chance for Strength I
  * 40% chance for Speed I
  * 30% chance for Regeneration I
  * 20% chance for Health Boost II
  * 10% chance for Resistance I
* Zombies and their variants now always have a weapon and some armor; the change to drop gear has been dropped to 1.5%
* All deep underground areas are now Deep Dark
* Shriekers no longer have 3 warnings (so wardens will always spawn)
* Wardens now have their own mob cap
* Creepers, husks, and parched now spawn in the Deep Dark
* Witches spawn 3 times as often in normal hostile mob spawns since I know they are a pain to deal with
* Things that should prevent you from making a mob switch easily:

  * Hostile mobs can not be renamed
  * Hostile mobs can not enter boats
  * Hostile mobs that pick up items will not be prevented from despawning; also they only have a 1.5% chance to drop those items
* Most loot tables now really suck
* Some trial spawners have been added randomly to structures, just for fun
* Stone blocks not adjacent to air are now infested
* Ore now only generates when adjacent to air; ancient debris is the exception to this, but it will be surrounded with infested netherrack; it also no longer generates adjacent to lava
* I've added infested tuff, andesite, diorite, granite, calcite, blackstone, and netherrack
* Infested blocks spawn withers instead of sivlerfish; these withers don't drop nether stars; you'll have to farm skulls and spawn a normal wither for that; there is also a limit of 4 withers per chunk, to lower the change of the game crafting; also infested blocks broken by explosions / withers don't spawn withers
* Wolves now have 1 hp, so you have to give them armor
* Squid spawn is replaced with glowsquid
* Cod and salmon spawn are replaced with tropical
* Ocean monuments no longer have gold blocks or sponges
* Ruined portals and bastions no longer have gold blocks; they have been replaced with yellow glazed terracotta
* Woodland mansions no longer have diamond/gold/etc. blocks; they have been replaced with glazed terracotta
* Illusioners now spawn in raids, and have totems of undying
* Evokers and illusions now only have a 15% chance to drop their totem; otherwise, they use the totem
* Villagers, illagers, zombies (and their variants), and skeletons (and their variants) now pickup totems if possible and will use them
* Totems used by mobs (not by the player) are buffed; they give:

  * Resistance III (for 8:00)
  * Regeneration III (for 2:00)
  * Absorption VI (for 1:00:00)
  * Fire Resistance (for 1:00:00)
  * Invisibility (for 2:00)
* Villages are always abandoned; and churches no longer have brewing stands
* Any villager spawn is now replaced with the zombie villager, forcing you to cure them
* Bookshelves in structures have been replaced with the useless chiseled bookshelf block; also librarian sells chiseled bookshelf instead of normal bookshelf
* Books are now very expensive; one book requires a rabbit hide block, 3 compressed paper blocks, a diamond, an emerald, an amethyst shard, a dripleaf, and a glowstone block; good luck!
* Strongholds have been completely removed
* Flint and steal now requires sniffer flowers, just to make you light your Nether portal some other way
* All types of armor and tools now require a turtle scute in order to be crafted
* Lots and lots of creaking hearts (with logs) are now in the ground, everywhere
* Beds and respawn achors no longer explode, since that would make mining too easy
* Fishing rods now only have 10 durability, and fishing loot is nerfed, but you can still get Mending from it if you really want to
* Books can no longer be disenchanted by the grindstone
* Going below Y=-63 in the Overworld or Y=1 in the Nether or End kills you
* Going above Y=127 in the Nether kills you
* There are no book and quills, so you can't easily regenerate chunks
* There is a limit of 16 bees per chunk, to prevent them from being used to easily regenerate chunks
* Similarly, there is a limit of 16 sulfur cubes per chunk
* TNT can on longer be used on sulfur cubes, because the bug related to that might somehow be useful
* Scaffolding cannot be waterlogged and is broken by water
* Doors and trapdoors are now waterlogged so you can't use them to cheese the water
* Phantoms can't die but despawn in the day; and they spawn every night even when you sleep
* Redstone dust and observers no longer work, and redstone components can't even be crafted, just to discourage redstone from being used
* Wither roses no longer give the wither effect

The mod is intended to played on a custom world, where:

* There are some extra surprises sprinkled here and there
* Only the circle of chunks containing where all the strongholds would be is generated
* New chunks cannot generate and trying to enter them kills you; which also makes chunk regeneration pretty infeasible
* There is one End portal in the center of the world and it is heavily guarded, basically as a giant vault; with tons of wardens and elder guardians with special tags that prevent from despawning and Resistance V so they can't die
* The goal is to get to the End, which is pretty tame... I just removed the entire End Island and added 100 extra end crystals; pretty tame since you can just out-DPS the dragon's regen; the dragon is not given random status effects; it's supposed to be immune to status effects anyways
* Withers and wardens in the End despawn instantly, to discourage using them against the dragon

Good luck beating this challenge without cheating. With lots of patience and time it should be doable, but difficult.
