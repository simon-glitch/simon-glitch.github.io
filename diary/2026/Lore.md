# Lore

Info:
* Lore is blue by default.
* Some redstone lore is red
* Curse of Binding and Curse of Vanishing show up as red, while other enchantments are blue
* Music disc and goat horn lore is a light gray color
* Only the Items tab has lore describing what the block is used for
* Potions/Tipped Arrows list the effects of the item (one effect per line), and then how the effects change the attributes of the recipient; if there are no effects, the lore says "No Effects"
* Enchanted items list the enchants of the item (if any; one effect per line), and then how the enchants change the attributes of the recipient
